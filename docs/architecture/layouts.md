# Room and shelf layouts (1.58.0; prototype #131)

Notes for the next session. What it is for, with pictures and Ethan's answers, is in `docs/prototypes/layouts.md`; how to use it is in `manual/layouts.md`.

## The model
Everything on a layout is a box in inches: a place (`x` from the left, `y` from the back, `z` off the floor: its left, back, bottom corner) and a size (`w`, `d`, `h`), with shape `box` or `round` (a circle from above). Kinds: `plant`, `light`, `shelf`, `other`, `zone` (an area to keep clear). The top view draws x and y, the front view x and z. A view in three dimensions needs nothing more stored.

## Server: `app/plantdb/layouts.py` (`ORDER = 66`)
- Tables `layouts` and `layout_items`, made on first use by `layout_tables(c)` (which also adds `hours` and `dim_pct` to a table made before them). Nothing in `init_db()`: when the prototype becomes a release, the tables move there.
- `layout_start(c, area, spot)`: what a layout of a place starts with. A shelf: its width and depth, its levels as locked boards 0.75 in thick (`layout_boards`, from the measurements `levels` and `gaps_in` or `gap_in`), and 24 in of headroom. A room: 144 by 120 in (ceiling from its measurements, else 96) with its spots as blocks along the back wall.
- `layout_item_dict(c, it)`: a thing as the page gets it. What only the record knows is added here:
  - a plant: `page_h` (its page's `height_in`; `h` is that when the page has one), `need` (`plant_need`), `rec` and `rec_label` (where the record has it; a place is a `pid`: a spot's id, or minus an area's id);
  - a light: `pts` (its maker's figures), `dims`, `hours`, `dim_pct`, `near_in`, `far_in`, `owned`;
  - one of the record's shelves: `place`, and `levels` (`layout_levels`: `[{n, name, z, room}]`, the height of each level's top above the shelf's foot and the clear height above it) with `board_in`.
- Routes: `GET/POST /api/layouts`, `GET/PUT/DELETE /api/layouts/<id>`, `POST /api/layouts/<id>/items`, `PUT/DELETE /api/layouts/items/<id>`, `POST /api/layouts/<id>/record`.
- **What a layout changes in the record**, both through `apply_changes()` (a version, History, the home limit):
  - a plant's `height_in`, when the item PUT carries `h` (`layout_plant_height`; `page_h` in the body says what the page is to say instead, which Undo uses, and `null` takes the height off the page);
  - a plant's home, area and spot, in `layout_record` (`{moves: [{plant, place}]}`; every move is checked before any is made).
- A layout belongs to its place's home (`layout_home_check`). `test_home_limit.py` tries all five routes, and a height and a move made through a layout for a plant at another home.

## Page: `app/static/js/layouts.js`, `app/static/css/layouts.css`
State is `LY`; the drawings are SVG; one `addFeature()` call adds the page, the buttons on Rooms and spots and the line on a plant's page.

- **`lyFindings(L)`** is the one place that decides every mark. Hard: `outside`, `overlap`. Soft: `tight`, `nearlight`, `zone`, `floating`, `count`, and the light kinds `toodim`, `dim`, `bright`, `toobright` (`lyIsLv`). It returns the list with the light Map on it (`F.light`).
- **Shelves with levels.** A shelf item with `levels` is not solid: its boards are. `lySolids(b)` gives a thing's solid parts, `lySurf(b)` the places on it to stand, `lyRoom(t, b)` the room above a thing, and `lyUp(a, b)` the gap between the nearest solid parts of two things. `lyUnder`, `lyOver`, `lyFree`, `lyPlace`, `lyStops` and the light's "something in the way" all go through these, so a plant can stand on a level inside a block, be too tall for it, or be lit through its open side.
- **Carrying** (Ethan, November 15: "Plants should be carried with shelves"). `lyRiders(L, it)` is what a thing carries: what stands on any of its surfaces or hangs under one of its solid parts, and what those carry; not a locked thing, not a zone. A move starts with `lyCarryStart` (each rider with its place), and `lyCarryMove(it, from, riders)` gives each rider the thing's own shift. The arrow keys (`LY.nudge.riders`), a drag (`LY.drag.riders`) and the typed boxes and "Stands on" (`lyset`) all do this, and hand the riders to `lyCommit`, which makes one Undo entry and saves each thing. `lyLand`, `lyPutOn`, `lyStops` and `lyStandOpts` take the riders as `skip`: what a thing carries is not in its own way and is nothing for it to land on.
- **Landing.** `lyLand(L, it, was, skip)` runs after a sideways move (a drag or the arrow keys in the top view, a typed place): a thing that rested on something stays at its height while still over it, goes onto the shelf or thing now under its middle (the lowest level it fits on that is free), or down to the floor. A thing in the air keeps its height. `lyPutOn(L, it, q)` puts a thing on one chosen surface: "Stands on" lists every shelf and level (`lyStandOpts`).
- **The front view, one wall at a time** (Ethan, November 15). On a layout that is not one spot's (`lyWalled`: a room, an area, a space of one's own) the front view looks at `LY.wall`: `back`, `left`, `right`, `front`, `mid` (the things away from the walls) or `all` (everything; a spot's layout is always that). `mid` and `all` face the way `back` does (`lyFaceOf`).
  - `lyWallsOf(L, it)`: the walls a thing is at: each one it is within `LY_WALL_IN` (24 in) of; a thing that stands on or hangs under another is where that one is; a thing far from every wall gets `['mid']` and is in no wall's view (Ethan, November 15). `lyWallSet(L, wall)` is the set a view draws; it goes to the drawing as `o.only`, with `o.face` (the wall faced).
  - `lyAc(L, it, face)` and `lyAw(it, face)`: where a thing is across the view and how wide; `lyAcrossW` the wall's width; `lyNear` how near the viewer (the paint order). `lyItTf`, `lyGeo`, `lySelMark`, `lyLabSpots`, `lySvg` and `lyMeasure` use them. In `lyNudge` and `lyDragMove` the sideways move is along x or y, turned round at the left and front walls.
  - `lyWallFollow(it)` takes the view to a thing's wall. It is called when a thing is chosen, put on, or moved (also by Undo and Redo) while it is the chosen one; a wall picked by hand stays until then.
  - The chooser (`#ly-wall`, action `lywall`) is in the front view's own figure, not in the tools row: at the largest text on a phone the tools already fill the screen. `lyDrawViews` gives it the keyboard back when it redraws.
  - `lyNameClash(L, o)`: in a front view a name that would run into a nearer thing's name, or that a nearer thing stands in front of, is left out (`o.noname`).
  - The top view marks the wall looked at (`o.wallmark`, `.ly-wallmark`) when both views show.
- **`lyLight(L)`**: each plant's daily light from the drawn lights. `lyLightOn` takes the light from a light's underside if it hangs and from its top if it stands, reads its figure at the straight-line distance to the plant's top (`lyPar` is `lights.py`'s `_loglog`), multiplies by the square of (how far below over how far away), and by the light's hours and brightness. A board between shuts it out. `lyVerdict` is `light_verdict`. A plant is `reached` at 1 mol/m² a day or straight under a light that is on; only then does it get a verdict and a mark. Every plant carries a sign once a drawn light has figures (`lyLvAt`, `.ly-lvb`): its verdict's, or a grey dash (`none`) when no light reaches it.
- **The record.** `lyRecTo(L, it)` says where the drawing puts a plant; `lyRecList` the plants the record has elsewhere; action `lyrec` moves them.
- **Undo and Redo.** `LY.undo` and `LY.redo` hold `{label, short, undo, redo}`. `lyUndoPush` and `lyCommit` add one and empty `LY.redo`. A thing that is taken off and put back gets a new id: `LY.ids` maps the old to the new and `lyCur(id)` follows it, so older entries still find the thing. Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y and Ctrl+Shift+Z are heard by the whole page except in a box being typed in.
- **Two locks** (Ethan, November 16). `locked` keeps a thing's place (drag by its middle, the arrow keys, `LY_PLACE_KEYS`: x, y, z, Stands on, Hangs under); `size_locked` keeps its size (the corner handle, `LY_SIZE_KEYS`: w, d, h, shape). Turn a quarter needs both off. `layout_tables` adds the column and copies `locked` into it, because before it `locked` held both. A thing locked in place does not ride on what it stands on (`lyRiders`).
- **Saving.** `lyCommit` draws, keeps the change for Undo and sends it (`lySaveItem`, chained on `LY.q`). `lyset` reads a box's value before it waits for earlier saves. A failed save shows the layout as it is saved (`lyNotSaved`).
- **Sizes on screen.** `lyMeasure()` sets each view's scale and the arrangement (`one`, `stack`, `pair`, `tall`). On a shelf unit the top view shows one level (`lyLevelNow`).

## Added on November 17, 2026 (1.58.0; Ethan: "I would like to have all of these implemented please!")
- **Windows** (kind `window`, columns `facing` and `day_dli`, added by `layout_tables`). `LAYOUT_FACING`, `LAYOUT_WINDOW_DLI` (the starting daylight at the glass for each way), `LAYOUT_WIN_FALL_IN` (24: half as much that far away), `LAYOUT_SHADE` (0.5). A window is "flat": nothing stands on it, it carries nothing, and it overlaps nothing (`lyFlat`, `solid` in `lyFindings`).
- **The light sums, page and server alike:** `lyDayOn` / `layout_day_on` (the wall is the nearest, `lyWinWall`; the nearest point of the opening; daylight × cos / (1 + (dist/24)²)); `lyCross` / `_ly_cross` with `lySegPlan` / `_ly_seg_plan` (exact, so a thin board is not missed) for what is in the way; `lyShade` / `_ly_shade` (each plant crossed lets half through, for lights and windows). `lyLight` gives `day`, `wins`, `wshut`, `shade` and `lit` (reached by a light, or 1 mol of daylight); the verdict is for `lit`. `reached` stays lights only, as Grow lights uses it. **Change both copies together:** `lightsread_check.py` and `layoutsmore_check.py` compare them.
- Grow lights: a reading carries `day`, `wins`, `shade`; `drawn_dli` adds `day`; `lgLine` says the daylight and the shade.
- **Light map** (`LY.lmap`, `LY.lmapAt`, kept in the device's own choices): `lyLightAt(L, x, y, z)` for a point; `lyLightMap` (about 40 squares across), `lyLmapSvg` (squares in six bands, `LY_LBANDS`, and numbers spaced 2.9 rem apart), `lyLmapKey`. The top view is drawn again whole while a thing moves when the map is on (`lyLive`).
- **3D view** (view `3d`; `LY.az`, `LY.el` kept for each layout): `ly3dBasis` (right, up and toward-the-viewer vectors), `ly3dPieces` (boxes, 16-sided round ones, a shelf's boards and posts, a zone on the floor), sorted far to near by their middles; `ly3dSvg`, `ly3dFigure` (Turn left and right by 30°, higher and lower by 15° from 15° to 75°). Only the first piece of a thing takes the keyboard. `lySelMark` draws nothing in 3D (`is-sel` makes it heavier); `ly3dDir` turns an arrow key into a step in the top view; the pointer only chooses there. `lyLive` draws the 3D view again whole.
- **A group** (`LY.many`, with `LY.sel` leading): `lyWith`, `lyGroup`, `lyGroupRiders` (the rest of the group and what each carries ride along, so the arrow keys and a drag move them all), `lyGroupHtml`, `lyLineUp` (`LY_LINE`, and spreading with equal gaps), one Undo entry through `lyCommit`'s `words`. Shift, Ctrl or Cmd with a press, Shift with Enter, or the list's "With the chosen thing".
- **A shelf's measurements:** a shelf unit's layout carries `sheet`, and a record shelf on a room's layout carries its `sheet`. `lySheetDiff` compares the boards (levels, clear heights, width, depth, room above) or the block's width and depth; `lySheetSave` reads the whole sheet (`GET /api/rooms/<spot>`), changes those keys and PUTs it, with Undo.
- **Offline:** `offline.js` lets `PUT /api/layouts/items/<id>` and `PUT /api/layouts/<id>` wait in the outbox, says them as "A layout: NAME moved", and patches the saved layout answers. `lySaveItem` sends `_what` (the name, for that list; the server leaves it) and counts what was kept (`LY.offn`, said in the status box). `/api/offline/manifest` lists the layouts.
- Check: `dev/test/layoutsmore_check.py` (`run_checks.py layoutsmore`).

## Checks
- `dev/test/unit/test_layouts.py` and the layout cases in `test_home_limit.py`.
- **A lesson from a step that failed once in four runs.** The step typed two boxes and waited for a mark that the first box alone already produced, then read the place: when the first box's save was slow, the second box had not been taken yet. `dev/tmp/ly/race.py` (not kept in the repository) showed it failing 10 times in 10 with saves held back 400 ms, on the page before and after any fix. Wait for everything a step expects in one `until()`. The same hunt found a real fault in the page (a box read after waiting), which has its own step now: three boxes typed while saves are held back.
- `dev/test/layouts_check.py` (in `run_checks.py` as `layouts`; it takes a base address): the geometry, the light, landing and the record are each asked directly with made-up things, then tried on the page; a viewer; a phone in the dark theme at the largest text.

## Grow lights reads a layout (1.58.0; prototype #141)
Ethan chose on November 16, 2026: the plant's mark is the layout's; a light's hours and brightness are set on the layout; a plant under a light on a layout but in no spot counts as having a light.

- **Server.** `layouts.py` has the light sum too (`layout_light`, `layout_light_on` and the `_ly_*` geometry), the same as `lyLight()` in `layouts.js`. `lightsread_check.py` compares the two for every drawn plant: keep them in step. `layout_lights_for_grow(c)` goes into core's `LIGHTS_FROM_LAYOUTS` and gives, for every layout of a place, the lights drawn there (the first of each light) and each drawn plant's reading.
- **Grow lights** (`lights.py`): `from_layouts(c)` reads that list. `spot_layout(place, light, LL)` is the drawn light a spot follows. `lights_state` gives such a spot the drawn light's hours and brightness and a `layout` ({id, name, item}); it gives `drawn` (plants in no spot that a drawn light reaches) and `readings`; the suggested layout leaves the `drawn` plants out. `spot_tie_body` makes a spot saved on Grow lights take the layout's hours and brightness.
- **The layout writes through.** When a drawn light's hours or brightness change (`layout_item_one`), `layout_light_to_spots` sets them on the light spots of the layout's place with that light, with a History line. Only from the first of that light on the layout, the one Grow lights reads.
- **The server decides each drawn plant's mark** (1.58.0, Ethan: "I would like all 3 ideas implemented"). `reading_for(LL, code, pid)` picks a plant's reading (the layout of the spot's place, else of the plant's own place, else one where a light reaches it). `read_rows` gives each drawn plant of a spot its `layout` reading and the layout's mark (`verdict`), and keeps the spot's own as `spot_verdict` and `spot_word`. So `better_spot` ("It would suit ...") goes by the layout's mark. A plant no drawn light reaches has the verdict `none` (`LIGHT_NONE`, "No light reaches it").
- **A reading** (`layout_reading`): `lid`, `lname`, `pid`, `item`, `name`, `here`, `dli`, `verdict`, `reached`, `on` (each light that gives it light: `light_id`, `name`, `up`, `side`, `dli`), `parts` (each drawn light that shines on it unblocked, with `par` at full brightness whatever its hours) and `shut` (names of lights something stands in the way of).
- **Best setting** (`best_setting(c, L, units, plants, place)`): when L is drawn on the layout of `place`, a plant drawn there gets `drawn_dli(reading, L, hours, dim)` at each setting tried (its `parts` of that light scaled, the other lights as they are). The distance is only for the plants not drawn (`None` when every plant is drawn). The answer has `layout` (`items`: every drawn L on that layout, `same` when the layout already has the best) and `each`. In the dialog, **Set ... on the layout** (`spotbestlay`, `spotBestLayout()`) PUTs the hours and brightness to each of those items, and `layout_light_to_spots` brings the spot along.
- **The spot dialog's preview** (`/api/lights/preview`) takes the layout's hours and brightness when the light is drawn on the layout of the chosen place, and the drawn plants' marks from that layout only.
- **Compare lights** (`compare.py`): `layout_swap(L, light_id, Ln)` gives every drawn `light_id` light row Ln's figures (same box, same hours; its brightness or Ln's next step up), and `layout_try(c, lid, light_id, Ln)` each drawn plant now and then. `GET /api/lights/<id>/try?item=ID` tries a light in place of a drawn one; `?spot=ID` does it for the spot's drawn plants when its light is drawn on its place's layout. `/api/lights/compare` lists `drawn` (the lights Grow lights reads) for the Where list.
- **Page.** `lights.js` draws it all from the server's data: `lgLine` (the line under a drawn plant), `lgSpotLine` (the paragraph in a spot), `lgNote` (above the spots, with the plants lit in no spot from `LT.drawn` and the layouts from `LT.read_layouts`), `lgPlant` (on a plant's page, from `/api/lights?plant=CODE`'s `layout` and `drawn`), and "N read from a layout" in a spot's summary. `layoutlights.js` is gone; its styles stay in `css/layoutlights.css` (with `.lv-none`).
- **A shorter Grow lights (1.58.0; Ethan chose option B on November 16, 2026; the preview setting `plantdb.ltopt` is gone):** each light, suggested spot, room and the list with no light left is a `details.lt-spot-fold` (ids `L<light>`, `P<index>`, `R<room>` and `U`, kept in `plantdb.ltspots` with the spots'). Inside an open light `.lt-tight` makes each fact one line and the maker's figures a list (`css/lights-compact.css`; the old table `.lt-ppfd` is gone). **Open every** and **Close every** act on their own section. Checks that read inside a light, room or suggested spot open the folds first (`lights`, `mounting`, `pots`). Gallery he chose from: https://claude.ai/artifact/V6SL6vDeP9LJTFA7CnNSWH
- Check: `dev/test/lightsread_check.py` (`run_checks.py lightsread`).
