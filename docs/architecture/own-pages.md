# Features with files of their own: voice search, frost dates, repotting, the sitter sheet, care cards

Part of the notes on how Plant record is built. The short map is `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; this file holds the detail for one area, moved here word for word on November 15, 2026.

## Notes
- **Search by voice (prototype proto/voice-search):** a button beside every `input[type=search]` (added by a MutationObserver in `app/static/js/voice.js`, loaded after `notify.js`) records with `getUserMedia` + `MediaRecorder` (WebM/Opus or MP4/AAC), stops after about 1.5 s of quiet (an `AnalyserNode` on the stream: RMS above three times the first 0.3 s, at least 0.015), after 15 s, or on a second press, and posts the recording to `POST /api/voice`.
  - **App** (`app/plantdb/voice.py`): `POST /api/voice` (field `audio`, at most 2 MB; in `VIEWER_POSTS`, it changes nothing) forwards it with `voice_prompt()` (page names, active plants' common names without their brackets, then how codes look; Whisper keeps the end of a long prompt) to `PLANTDB_SPEECH_URL` (default `http://speech:8093`) and returns `{text}`. `GET /api/voice/status` passes on the service's `/status` (or `state: off` with the reason). `speech_call()` turns 503 / no connection / timeouts into plain words.
  - **Speech service** (`speech/service.py`, `speech/Dockerfile`, `speech/requirements.txt`, compose service `speech`, container `plant-speech`): Flask + gunicorn (1 worker) on port 8093, compose network only, read-only root, `cap_drop: ALL`, mounts only `./data/speech:/cache`. faster-whisper 1.2.1 on CTranslate2, CPU, `compute_type=int8`, `cpu_threads` = `SPEECH_THREADS` or every core. `SPEECH_MODEL` (from `PLANTDB_SPEECH_MODEL`, default `small.en`) downloads into `/cache/models` on first start; `/status` reports `setting up` with MB done (the folder's size against `SIZES`), then `ready`; `PLANTDB_SPEECH=0` gives `state: off`. `POST /transcribe` decodes with PyAV in memory (`av` held at 18.1: 19 breaks faster-whisper 1.2.1), `language='en'`, `beam_size=2`, `vad_filter`, `initial_prompt`, one at a time (`LOCK`). Nothing is written to disk.
  - **Front end:** `vsClean()` (codes, extra words, "open"), `vsPage()` (`PAGES_LIST` plus `VS_PAGE_NAMES`), `vsOpen()` (a code, then a page, then one plant by name), `vsFinish()`; the Heard line is spoken with `readAloudText()` unless Display's `vsearch` is `quiet`; `vsearch: '0'` hides `.vs-wrap` (`html[data-vsearch="0"]`) and turns off V. The service's state shows under the picture checker's on Identify (`#vs-st`) and in Display (`#vs-dv-st`). The browser's own SpeechRecognition is not used for search (the 1.34.0 "Speak" dictation button in notify.js still uses it).
  - **1.44.0:** the Speak buttons (`notify.js`, `dictateStart()` / `dictSend()`) record the same way (3 seconds of quiet, a minute at most) and post with `mode=note`, which gives Whisper a prompt for a note (plant names, written as sentences); `micErrText()` in voice.js words a microphone that did not start. notify.js starts them on `DOMContentLoaded` because voice.js loads after it. The speech service takes up to 75 seconds.
  - **Tests:** `dev/test/mock_speech.py` (started by `mock_server.py` on `MOCK_SPEECH_PORT`, default 8093; `/mock/say`, `/mock/state`, `/mock/last`), `dev/test/voice_check.py` (a stand-in microphone: an oscillator the test switches on and off, so the real recording, quiet detection and upload run; Chromium with `--autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required`), `dev/test/unit/test_voice.py`.
- **Frost dates (1.45.0, prototype #65):** `app/plantdb/frostdates.py` works out, from the stored daily lows, the usual (median) first and last 36°F, 32°F and 28°F nights per winter (July to June), earliest and latest, days to go, this winter so far and each plant's photo; shown in `weather.js` (Weather page section, Cold-night checklist groups, Back outside's "When the cold nights usually end") and as one line on Today in October/November and February/March (needs 3 whole winters). Check: `dev/test/frostdates_check.py`; unit tests in `test_server_pure.py`.
- **Repotting (1.50.0, prototype #70 rebuilt on the registry):** `app/plantdb/repotting.py` (`repot_auto()` from the growth rate, `repot_plan()` pure, `repot_report()` on `plants_at(c, location(c))`; `GET/PUT /api/repotting`; the only stored thing besides `care_log` rows of kind `repot`/`prep_repot` is the setting `repot_choice`, JSON {code: months | 'off'}); `js/repotting.js` with one `addFeature()` call; `css/repotting.css` (its viewer rule included). Checks: `dev/test/unit/test_repotting.py`, `dev/test/repotting_check.py`.
- **Plant-sitter sheet (1.43.0, prototype #41):** `app/static/sitter.html` + `sitter.js`, a page of its own like the care cards, served by `GET /api/sitter` (`app/plantdb/sitter.py`, reusing `water_report`, `frost_report`, `group_codes`). The links on Today and group pages are registered by `js/main.js` (1.46.0; before that a MutationObserver). `sw.js` `shellKey()` saves `.html` pages under their own path, so opening a print page offline no longer replaces the saved app shell. Check: `dev/test/sitter_check.py`.
  - **By link (1.49.0, PLAN.md step 4c):** the sheet's renderer moved to `static/sitter-sheet.js` (`window.PlantSitterSheet = { sheet(D, OPT, X), dayName, dayShort, esc, iso, addDays }`), shared by `sitter.html` and `share.html`. Tables `sitter_shares(token, from_d, to_d, group_id, place_id, note, photos, created_by, created_at, expires_at, revoked_at, last_seen)` and `sitter_ticks(token, day, plant_code, kind, ticked_at)` (`SITTER_SCHEMA`, run by `init_db()`). `POST /api/sitter/shares` (editors and the owner; `secrets.token_urlsafe(24)`, expires at the end of the day after `to_d` in the home's time zone, records `location(c)` as the place), `GET /api/sitter/shares` (this place's links, newest first, `live`, `url` from the calendar's `public_base`, `local_url`), `DELETE /api/sitter/shares/<token>` (sets `revoked_at`; ticks kept), `GET /api/sitter/ticks?from&to&group` (the ticks of every live link for those days, so the printout shows them). The link holder reaches only `/share/<token>` (the page, served with its status even when dead so the reason reads in plain words), `/share/<token>/data` (the sheet at the link's place via `place_for()`, `before=[]`, the owner's units, photo paths rewritten under the token), `POST /share/<token>/tick {day, plant, kind, on}`, `/share/<token>/photo/<file>` (only photos on the sheet), `/style.css` (fonts repointed to `/share/<token>/fonts/`), `/fonts/<name>`, `/share.js`, `/sitter-sheet.js`. `guard()` lets `^/share/[A-Za-z0-9_-]{20,}(/|$)` through for GET, HEAD and POST, like `/cal/` (1.58.0: both in `public_link()`, which is also all a Funnel visitor can reach; see "From the internet" under Access); `share_row()` answers 404 (unknown), 410 (taken down or expired; 410 now has a JSON body like 404). Funnel: `tailscale funnel --bg --https=8443 --set-path=/share http://127.0.0.1:8080/share`. Tests: `dev/test/unit/test_share.py`, `dev/test/sitlink_check.py`.
- **Care cards (1.19.0, 1.20.0):** a page of its own, `app/static/card.html` + `card.js` (no app.js; the printout carries only the cards).
  - URLs: `/card.html?plant=P-006` (or several, comma-separated), `?group=<id>`, `?all=1` (every active plant, from the Labels page).
  - Options are kept in localStorage `plantdb.card2`: `size` (`card` = 4 x 6 inch, the default, or `page`), `notes`, `photo`, `qr` and `pets` (on by default).
  - `card()` draws the full page and `card46()` the 4 x 6 card, with shorter wording (`row()`).
  - `pageSize()` writes the `@page` size into a `<style id="pc-page">`, because `@page` cannot depend on a class.
  - The links on plant and group pages are added by `careCardLinks()` in app.js.
  - Tested by `card_check.py`, which prints PDFs and checks font sizes and page counts.
