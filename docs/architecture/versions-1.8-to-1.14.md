# What 1.8.0 to 1.14.0 added (inside, audit log, console, about this plant)

Part of the notes on how Plant record is built. The short map is `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; this file holds the detail for one area, moved here word for word on November 15, 2026.

## Notes
- **1.8.0 additions:**
  - **Frost:** `frost_report()` turns the home forecast into cold nights and the outdoor plants at risk (`FROST_MARGIN` = close calls). `GET /api/frost`, `POST /api/frost/done`.
  - **Watering:** `water_report()` works out each plant's next check from its soil moisture range (or the `water_every_days` field), the last three days' highs and rain (0.25 in or more counts for Outdoors plants), and `care_log`. `GET /api/water`, `GET/POST /api/care`, `DELETE /api/care/<id>`.
  - **Timeline:** `GET /api/plants/<code>/timeline` groups the plant's `audit_log` rows into events and adds its finished requests.
  - **Copy for Claude:** `GET /api/for-claude`, `GET /api/for-claude.txt`, `POST /api/for-claude/copied`. Rows signed "Claude (...)" (upgrade batches) are left out.
  - **Photos:** `repair_seed_photos()` copies seed photos that are missing from the data folder, for installs whose photos arrived in packs.
- **1.9.0 additions:** getting plants ready to come inside. Plant fields `leaf_type` and `pot_size` (optional enums: `ENUMS`, `OPTIONAL_ENUMS`) and `pests_found` (list). `PREP_METHODS` holds the ten methods and their steps; `prep_rate()` rates each for a plant, `prep_plan()` adds progress, status and the keep-apart date. Steps are logged to `care_log` as `prep_*` kinds (a soak or repot counts as watering). `GET /api/prep`, `GET /api/plants/<code>/prep`, `POST /api/plants/<code>/prep-traits`.
  - **1.40.1:** care kind `prep_start` ("Started the move-inside checklist") marks a plan as started: an outside plant shows as `doing` before any step, and a plant already inside with a start on or after its move-in date goes through the checklist (`doing` while Best steps remain) and is kept apart from the start date. `prep_plan()` adds `started`, `inside`, `next_key` (the method the next step logs, when it can be done today) and `next_word`; `/api/prep` keeps a slim `log` per plant for Undo. Front end: `prepActs(D, where)` draws Start the checklist / the next step / Undo the start (plant page `.pr-top`, Coming inside rows), acts `prepstart` and `prepjump`, `prepFocus()` picks where focus goes after a redraw.
  - **1.41.1:** the steps are tick boxes: `prepChecklist(D, where)` (all methods in order; `prepRec()` is rank >= 2, the rest get `li.mi-not` and a "Not recommended" badge), change act `preptick` (ticking POSTs `prep_<key>`, unticking DELETEs the latest entry from the plan's `log`). The Coming inside page draws each plant as `details.mi-fold` (open state kept in `PREP.open`, act `prepfold`), with the steps' "How to do it" filled in from `D.methods`. Class prefix `mi-` because `pc-` belongs to the printed plant cards. `prepActs()` no longer draws the next-step button. `/api/lights/places/<int(signed=True):i>`: a room itself is minus its area id.
  - **1.42.0:** `viewLights()` draws `#lt-top` (sticky below the top bar, `--lt-top-h` measured by `ltTopMeasure()`, act `lttop`, remembered in `plantdb.lttop`) with the summary table `table.lt-sum-tbl` (stacked on phones from its cells' `data-h`). Section titles with a button use `<header class="sec-head">` (sticky like `section.blk > h2`). Your light spots are `details.lt-spot-fold` (open ones remembered in `plantdb.ltspots`, act `ltspotsall`); `ltSpotBody()`, `ltPlantLi()` (code and range on lines of their own, `ul.lt-plants.lt-two` two to a row). Rooms: `ltRoomsBlock()` puts the room's own New Shelf / Rename / Remove room by its title (the room itself is the place with no `name`) and gives each shelf its own buttons; act `shelfnew` → `roomDialog(null, {room})`, titled Build-A-Room.
- **1.10.0 additions:** the Audit log page and console. `audit_query(c, args)` builds the filtered query (`q` searches notes, values, names, codes and field labels with `LIKE … ESCAPE`; `plant` (`-` = no plant), `who` (`~claude`, `~people` or a name), `kind` (a key of `AUDIT_KINDS`), `field`, `from`/`to` (local dates, turned into UTC with the home time zone), `after` (ids above N, oldest first), `order`). `audit_rows()` adds `label` (`audit_label()`) and `action_word` (`ACTION_WORD`). Routes: `GET /api/audit` (newest first; `?after=ID` for the live console), `GET /api/audit/search` (`{entries, total, offset, limit, max_id}`, up to 500 a page), `GET /api/audit/facets` (what the filters can choose from), and `GET /api/audit.csv`, which takes the same filters.
- **1.11.0 additions:**
  - **Place in use:** `location(c)` is now the place the browser chose, sent as the `X-Place` header on every request (falling back to the home place). `home_place(c)` is the home place, used outside requests (hourly updates). `req_loc()` falls back to `location(c)`. Everything that used the home place (frost, watering, prep weather, current conditions, `local_today`, `/api/reference`, `/api/meta`) follows the header. `/api/meta` returns `place`, `places` and `climate` (`place_climate()`: a usual winter's coldest night and coldest-month night, from 3+ years of `weather_daily`, cached 6 hours). `/api/reference` adds `applies` (the written notes are for Round Rock, `REF_PLACE`) and `normals` (`place_normals()`, monthly averages from history).
  - **Fertilizer needs:** plant fields `npk_ratio` (text, normalised to `a-b-c` by `parse_ratio()`) and `fert_strength` (optional enum `FERT_STRENGTHS`). Table `fertilizers` (saved products). `fert_products()` merges saved ones with every product in `fertilizer_log`; `fert_match()` compares N-P-K as shares of the total (largest difference: ≤0.05 Matches, ≤0.12 Close, ≤0.22 Usable); `fert_dose()` aims the strongest nutrient at `STRENGTH_PPM` (150/75/55/40 ppm) and rounds to spoon measures (`nice_amount()`). Routes: `GET/POST /api/fertilizer/products`, `PUT/DELETE /api/fertilizer/products/<id>`, `GET /api/fertilizer/needs[?plant=]`.
- **1.14.0: about this plant.**
  - 23 new plant fields at the end of `FIELDS` (`growth_habit`, `kingdom`, `taxon_order`, `family`, `genus`, `height_ground`, `width_ground`, `height_pot`, `width_pot`, `growth_rate`, `lifespan`, `zone_low`, `zone_high`, `zone_notes`, `native_range`, `conservation_status`, `invasive`, `bloom`, `fruit`, `danger_humans`, `danger_animals`, `propagation`, `sources`). `init_db()` adds the columns. `sources` is a list field, one `Title | URL` per line.
  - `usda_zone(f)` (half zones of 5°F from 1a at -60°F), `zone_num()`, and `place_zone(c, L)`, which uses `place_climate()`'s `winter_low` and, within 0.25° of `REF_PLACE`, the 2023 map's "8b to 9a". `/api/meta` returns it as `zone`.
  - Front end: `aboutBlock()` in `<section id="about">`, `zoneHere()` (falls back to the colder end of the map zone when there is no weather history yet), `srcList()`, and the "About this plant" group in `GROUPS`.
  - `batchA5` fills every plant (guarded, old value ''). It is built by `dev/seed_src/build_batchA5.py` from `profiles_A5.py` (one profile per species or group, with `MAP` from plant code) and `sources_A5.txt` (`key|title|url`, links taken only from web search results).
