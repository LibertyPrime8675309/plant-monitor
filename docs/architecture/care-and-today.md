# Today, watering and care

Part of the notes on how Plant record is built. The short map is `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; this file holds the detail for one area, moved here word for word on November 15, 2026.

## Notes from the back end and front end lists
- **Watering by room and spot, and asking once (1.58.0):** Today's three watering lists (`care.js`: `waterByPlace()`, `wplGroups()`, `wplCard()`; styles in `css/today-places.css`) group the plants by area and spot in the order `WHERE()` gives, which is `areas.sort` within a side and `spots.sort` within an area; `POST /api/where/order` (`where.py`) moves one a place up or down by renumbering its group. `WATER_ORDER` (`localStorage plantdb.waterorder`: `place` or `late`) keeps the old rows one press away. A list is one grid (`.wpl-grid`): each spot is a `section.wpl-in` with `display:contents`, so its tile (`.wpl-head`) and its cards are cells of the same run. A card's picture is the label of a tick box (`data-act="wtick"`); the ticks of each list live in `WTICK` (memory only, emptied when the day changes) so they outlast a redraw. One bar per list (`.wpl-bar`, `wtickdo` in `events.js`) logs the ticked plants with one `POST /api/care` per kind: `data-kind` on the tick box is what Watered means for that plant (`water`, `topup`, `waterchange`), and Still moist (`check`) leaves pots kept in water out. The bar is `position:sticky` at the bottom, and `.wpl` itself has `display:contents` so the bar is held within the whole part, not within the list. `WATER_PICS` (`localStorage plantdb.waterpics`: `medium` or `large`; a Small size was dropped, and a device that had chosen it gets Medium) is `data-pics` on each `.wpl`, which only sets the grid's `--cw` (the width a card wants), `--lo` (the least it takes) and `--n` (how many a narrow screen still gets across). Someone who can only view gets the same cards with a link in place of the tick box, and no bar. Care never logged asks once (`plantdb/asks.py`, settings `ask_feed` and `ask_flush` = `{answer, on, by}`, `GET/PUT /api/asks`): no answer shows `askBlock()` on Today, `yes` counts a plant from `on` (`askOn()` in the page, `ask_started()` for the morning message in `push.py`), `no` drops plants with nothing logged. `askLine()` on the Fertilizer and Potting mixes pages changes the answer. Tests: `test_asks_order.py`, `test_today_1580.py`, `wplace_check.py`.
- **The first screen (1.58.0, `css/first-screen.css`):** All plants is headed by `plantsHead()` (`gallery.js`): count beside the title, one row of tools, one row of buttons; it is drawn for the width it has (`PL_NARROW`) and `plRedraw()` draws it again when the window crosses the narrow width. The winter-plan tiles (`ledger()`, `acts.plan`, `PLAN_COLOR`) are gone; the plan is a list (`data-f="pnplan"`). `frostBanner(false)` is one line (`.fz-line`); `frostBanner(true)` on Today is unchanged. On a plant's page `careQuick()` is a child of `main` straight after the header, `#quick-status` is short (`waterShort()` + `waterLast()`), `.pl-navrow` holds All plants, `pnavBlock()` and the layout switch, and feature modules add their care lines to `#quick-more` through `quickMore()` (`features.js`); `.quick` stays the name of the box with the buttons alone, so a page has one. On a narrow screen the top bar hides `.brand` and `.ver` unless More is open.

## The November 11 review, second part (1.57.0)
- **Notes on request:** `prefs.js` `notes` ('show' | 'ask', layout part, `data-notes` on `<html>`); Display group "Notes under buttons and boxes". In 'ask', `notesPass(root)` (ui.js; called after `addFoldButtons()` in `route()`, after feature additions, in `modal()` and on the `prefs` event) gives each `.hint` and `label.f > small` `data-note`, `role=button`, `tabindex=0`, `aria-expanded`; a capture click handler and Enter/Space/Escape toggle `.note-open`; a 550 ms touch press on a control opens the note `noteFor()` finds and swallows the following click. CSS (00-core.css, end) shrinks a closed note to font-size 0 with a "ⓘ Note" `::before` pill; hover on the note or its label/row opens it; print shows all.
- **Today:** Water's block is `keep` only when something is due soon; plants with no watering logged are the `today-unlogged` block (order 31, folded `details.grp`). **Plant page:** `PLANT_REF_FOLDED` (ui.js) seeds `FOLDS['#/plant']` once per browser (`plantdb.foldseed`; skipped under WebDriver unless `plantdb.foldseedtest`); the label card's top line links `#history` (act `jumpsec`), and the version line opens Page history; `.plant` columns 4fr/8fr. `.sec-inline` tools (a title further into its section) sit beside the title like `.sec-bare` ones (`foldMeasure()`).
- **Disabled buttons:** a `<span class="why" id>` after the button, `aria-describedby` on it; `.why` hides when the button is enabled (`:not([disabled]) + .why`); `.btn[disabled]` is dashed at full opacity.
- **Layout:** Display goes to `header.top .top-acts` on a page with no Navigation menu (`pinLayout()`); `navTop()` sets `--nav-top` to the top bar's bottom while `data-toptall`; on a phone `data-barhide` slides `#corner` away while scrolling down (not near the top or end, not while it has the focus). `focusTitle(app)` focuses the h1 (tabindex -1, no ring) on a new path. Hidden `h2.sr` headings fill the level gaps on All plants, Library and Year in review; the NAVITEMS scan skips `.sr`. Weather's `chartsEmpty()` shows `NO_READINGS()` instead of empty charts. Settings links Offline use, Backups and the Audit log. Library cards with no photo get `.nophoto-none`.
- **Saving mode:** `init_db()` reads `PLANTDB_JOURNAL` (DELETE by default; compose passes it from `.env`). WAL is used only if `wal_works()` (core.py) can write through one connection and read through another on `data/.wal-probe.db`; `JOURNAL` holds the mode and the probe's words, and `/api/health` carries them as `journal` (shown on Health when there is a note).
- **Dependencies:** `requirements.txt` pins exact versions; `.github/dependabot.yml` proposes weekly grouped updates for pip (`/`, `/speech`), Docker (`/`, `/vision`, `/speech`) and GitHub Actions.
- **Tests:** `test_core_jobs.py` (backup and restore, pruning, `backup_due()`, watering, frost levels, VPD, timestamps, daily light, local today, the WAL probe), `test_lint.py`, `test_js_names.py`; `dev/test/review2_check.py` for the pages.


## Bulk care at one home (1.58.0)
- `locations.tended` (INTEGER, default 1; added in place by `init_db()`): 0 = the home's plants are cared for outside the app. In `loc_dict()`, `place_brief()` (so `META.homes[].tended`), set by `PUT /api/places/<id>` `{tended}` (`site_fields()`) and by the owner's **Care** select on Homes and places (`siteCells()`, act `placesite`, key `tended`; not shown for the main home). Batches: `homes_add` takes `tended: false`; new `homes_update` [{lat, lon, fields: {tended: [old, new]}}] finds the home by its place and changes only a field still at `old`. Batch **D1** marks Baraboo (C9) as cared for outside the app (D1 sorts after C9; C10 would sort before C2).
- Front end (gallery.js): `homeTended(h)`, `careHome()` (the Home filter's home, else `placeHomeId()`), `waterAllList()` → {list, home, away, untended}; `drawGrid()` labels `#wa-btn` "Water all N shown at <home>", fills `#wa-why` (a `.why` reason when off) and `#wa-hint`; events.js `waterall` uses the same list and reports the server's `skipped`. `groupCare()` ticks only plants at the home in use that are tended (`#gc-away` says how many and where). care.js Today drops the "Watered all" / "I watered all" buttons at an untended home (`outH`).
- `POST /api/care` with no `done_on` dates each entry by the plant's own home (`home_today(c, home_id)` in care.py), not the browser's place.
- Tests: `dev/test/unit/test_water_homes.py`; `dev/test/water_check.py` (label, left-out note, Baraboo off with its reason, group ticks, the Homes and places select).

## Today tells the truth about tonight; small accessibility things (1.58.0; November 12 review, items 8 and 11)
- **Cold nights (weather.js):** `fzList(counts)`, `fzNights()` → `{ns, first, act, left}` (the nights with a plant on them; the first; the first whose level is not `watch`; `left(n)` = its plants not ticked as covered) and `fzToday()` → `{n, bit}` for Today. `frostBanner()` leads with `F.worst`, then gives `first`'s own counts when it is another night, and the deadline is `fzActBy(act.date)` (the evening before the first night that would damage a plant), with a sentence of its own for close calls only and for plants already covered. Before, the worst night's counts stood beside the first night's deadline. care.js `viewToday()`: `FZ = fzToday()`; the jump is `Cold night (${FZ.n})`; the first sentence lists only counts above zero.
- **Notifications (push.py):** `cold_words(counts)`; `push_cold()` read `n.get('worst')`, a key a night does not have, so every message said "are close calls". The evening reminder counts plants not ticked as covered and says when a night is close calls only. `push_due()` leaves out plants with no feeding logged (`p['last']`), as Today does.
- **Never logged (care.js, mixes.js):** `feedDue` splits into `feed` (a feeding is logged: counted) and `unfed` (none ever: block `today-unfed`, order 41, a folded `details.grp` with `fertlog` buttons; `fertlog` takes `data-codes`). mixes.js registers two Today blocks from one request (`mxCare()`): `mx-hydro` (items with `last`) and `mx-hydro-new` (order 34, folded). The Fertilizer page and the calendar file are unchanged.
- **Names (WCAG 2.5.3):** an `aria-label` starts with the words the control shows, then the detail ("Read aloud: the Water section", "Remove room: Garage", "Add this group: Aroids"). `paintWho()` sets the top bar button's label from its text. Calendar days: `label(d, short)` = what the day shows (`shown()`: number or short date, "today", badge texts), then `longDay(d)` and the cell's `aria`.
- **Colours:** light theme `--ink-2 #3b4b54`, `--moss #255738`, `--ochre #6a4600`, `--madder #87291e` (each at 7 to 1 or better as text on `--paper`, `--sheet` and its own soft tint); dark theme `--madder-soft #4c2522` and `--on-soft-link #badcf6`, used for plain links and outline buttons inside `.notice`, `.ask`, `.fz-banner`, `.sp-prev`, `.rule-prev`. `.id-view` (the camera box) is `#0b141a` in both themes; it was `var(--ink)`.
- **Scrolling tables:** `scrollBoxes(root)` (ui.js) gives a `.tbl-wrap` that overflows `tabindex=0`, `role=region` and a name from its caption, and takes them away when it fits. Called after a page is drawn (and 1.2 s later), after feature additions, in `modal()`, on `prefs`, on resize, and after the weather log and the lights comparison draw their tables.
- **Check:** `dev/test/names_check.py` visits every page in the top bar (the list comes from `SECTIONS`, so a new page is included) plus a plant, its edit page and six more, on a computer in the light theme and a phone in the dark theme, and fails on a control whose name leaves out its words or on text under 7 to 1; then the calendar day's name, the weather table from the keyboard, the camera box. `frost_check.py` puts made-up forecasts in the page (`FROST = ...`) and reads `frostBanner()` and `fzToday()`.
