# How often you water (prototype)

Part of the notes on how Plant record is built. The short map is `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; this file holds the detail for one prototype.

## How often you water (prototype proto/water-rhythm)
- `app/plantdb/rhythm.py` (ORDER 60; imports `water_base_days`, `water_factor`, `water_weather`, `RAIN_COUNTS_IN` from care.py). `rhythm_report(c, code=None)`: for active plants at the place in use (or one code), the days of `water`/`prep_soak`/`prep_repot` entries in the last `RHYTHM_WINDOW` (120) days, plus rain days (`rhythm_rain()`, weather_daily at `location(c)`) from the plant's first logged watering for Outdoors plants; gaps over `RHYTHM_MAX_GAP` (45) dropped; with `RHYTHM_MIN_GAPS` (3) or more, `gap` = the median and `state` = `rhythm_state(gap, every)` (`often` / `seldom` / `matches`; needs 25% and 2 days apart), else `few`. `every` is computed the same way `water_report()` does. `rhythm_setting_for(n, f)` picks the `water_every_days` whose `round(base * f)` lands nearest n. `GET /api/rhythm[?plant=]`; `PUT /api/rhythm {plant, every: N | null}` or `{plant, setting}` (Undo) changes `water_every_days` through `apply_changes()` (version, audit). Nothing new is stored and there is no schema change.
- `js/rhythm.js`: page `rhythm` (Today section, after Repotting; the Care row is full at 1280 px and one more page there makes the top bar too tall to stay put) and the add `rh-plant` (care: true) inside `#care-plant .verdict-box`; acts `rhset`, `rhclear`, Undo through `careUndoToast(msg, fn)`. Styles `css/rhythm.css` (with its viewer block). Checks: `dev/test/unit/test_rhythm.py`, `dev/test/rhythm_check.py`.
- **November 15, 2026.** Setting `water_exact` (a list of codes): `water_exact(c)` and `water_factor_for(exact, code, avg_high, inside)` in `care.py` give factor 1.0 to an indoor plant on the list, in `water_report()` and in `rhythm_report()`. `PUT /api/rhythm {plant, every}` for an indoor plant sets `water_every_days` to `every` and adds the plant; `{every: null}` and Undo (`{setting, exact}`) take it off. "Make all N of these match" is `rhall` in `rhythm.js`: one PUT per plant, one Undo.
