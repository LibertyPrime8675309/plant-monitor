# Grow lights, lights that fit the room, comparing lights, what is in each pot

Part of the notes on how Plant record is built. The short map is `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`; this file holds the detail for one area, moved here word for word on November 15, 2026.

## Notes from the back end and front end lists
- **1.13.0 additions (grow lights):**
  - **Tables:**
    - `lights(id, lkey, name, data JSON, count, fixture, notes, sort, ...)`. `data` holds the specs: `watts`, `ppfd` as `[[inches, PAR, area_sqft|null], ...]`, `estimated`/`area_estimated`, `dimming` (% steps), `timer_hours`/`timer_repeats`, `needs_socket`, `direction`, `photo`/`chart` (paths under `static/lights/`) with alt texts, and `url`.
    - `light_spots(id, name, light_id, units, distance_in, hours, dim_pct, notes, ...)`.
    - `light_assign(plant_code PK, spot_id)`: a plant is in at most one spot.
  - **Upgrade batches:** a batch can carry `lights_add`. A light is added once, by `key` or name, and Ethan edits it afterwards. `batchA3` (built by `dev/seed_src/build_batchA3.py` from `dev/photos-raw/lights`) adds his 11 lights and 4 requests.
  - **Maths:**
    - `ppfd_at()` and `area_at()` draw straight lines on log-log axes through the maker's points, extending the nearest segment (exponent 1.6 for a single point).
    - `dli_of()` is PAR × dim × hours × 0.0036.
    - `light_verdict()` gives too_dim (<80% of low), dim, good, bright, or too_bright (>130% of high).
    - `light_setting()` searches hours (12, 14, 16, 10, 8) and brightness steps for the distance (whole inches, within `light_range()`) that gives a target.
  - **Planner:** `light_plan()`.
    - Candidates are active plants inside or with winter plan Move indoors, not already in a spot. Highest target first.
    - A plant joins a suggested spot when the ranges overlap and the area allows (`area_at` × units × `LIGHT_ROOM` 1.5, against pot footprints from `pot_size`). Otherwise it opens one on the light with the lowest (strain, watts), where strain counts settings at 16 h or the closest distance.
    - A light is never used more times than its count. Spots are named "Bright/Medium/Low light N".
  - **Easing in:** `step_down()` gives week 1 at up to the group's high at 16 h, week 2 halfway, then the spot's setting. There is a porch step first when plants are out in the open and the outdoor light (`outdoor_dli()`: 7-day mean of `shortwave_radiation_sum` × 2.1, ignored above 75) is well above their high.
  - **Routes:**
    - `GET /api/lights` (`?plant=CODE` for the plant page) and `GET /api/lights/preview`
    - `POST /api/lights`, `PUT/DELETE /api/lights/<id>` (409 while a spot uses it)
    - `POST /api/lights/spots`, `PUT/DELETE /api/lights/spots/<id>`
    - Audit action `light`, kind "Grow lights".
  - **1.13.1:**
    - Table `indoor_places(id, room, name, sort)`: `name` is '' for the room itself. `light_spots.place_id` is added by `init_db()`.
    - Routes `POST /api/lights/places` and `PUT/DELETE /api/lights/places/<id>`. Removing a place sets its spots' `place_id` to NULL.
    - Spots carry `place`/`place_id`, and `lights_state()` returns `places`.
    - Lights have `data.model`.
    - Batches can carry `places_add` and `lights_update` (`{key, set: {field: [old, new]}}`, guarded; the fields are `fixture`, `notes`, `count` or any spec key). `batchA4` adds Ethan's rooms and answers.
    - Front end: `ltRooms()`, `ltRoomsBlock()`, `roomDialog()`, and the actions `roomnew`, `roomedit` and `roomdel`, which are in the viewer CSS block.
  - **Front end:** `viewLights()` (`#/lights`), `ltSpotCard()`, `ltLightCard()`, `spotDialog()` with live `spotPreview()` (`form[data-spot]`), `lightDialog()`, and `loadGrowPlant()` filling `#pl-grow` on plant pages. Change buttons (`spotnew`, `spotedit`, `spotdel`, `lightedit`, `lightnew`) are in the viewer CSS block.
  - **1.41.0:** `light_reach(d, pts)` (in `light_dict()` as `reach`: `near_in`, `far_in`, `ppfd_max`, `ppfd_min` at the lowest dimming, `dli12`, `dli_any` for 8 to 16 hours, `one_point`; never extended past the measured distances), `room_light` (from the kind), `suits` (candidates whose needs overlap `dli_any`). Candidates carry `photo`, `foot`, `inside`. `best_setting()` and `GET /api/lights/best` (whole inches in `light_range`, every hour in `LIGHT_HOURS` and dimming step; most Good, then most A bit dim/bright, then not crowded, 12 or 14 hours, full brightness, nearest the targets). `better_spot()` adds `better` to plants in spots (`lights_state`) and to `/api/lights/preview` (with `?spot=` to leave out the spot being edited). `make_spot()`; `POST /api/lights/spots/plan` sets up the whole plan; `POST /api/lights/spots/<id>/plants {add}`; `?plant=` returns `need` and `spots` with this plant's verdict. Front end: `ltSorted()`, `ltOptions()`, `spotLightCard()`, `spotRow()`, `spotFilter()` (search, Show and Sort on the rows; `ltVerdict()` mirrors `light_verdict()`), `spotBest()`, `growAddDialog()`; acts `spotadd`, `spotbest`, `spotplanall`, `growsug`, `growadd`, `grownew`. `:root[data-theme]` sets `color-scheme` so form controls follow the chosen theme.

## Compare lights (1.56.0, PLAN.md step 10)
- `app/plantdb/compare.py` (ORDER 64). `lights.status` ('owned' default, 'considering', 'retired'; added by `init_db`). A light that is not owned has count 0, so `light_plan` gives it no free units; `lights_state` lists only owned lights (and any still in a spot), so Grow lights and the spot dialog never offer a candidate. `light_status_set()` refuses to retire or un-own a light that is in a spot (409); buying sets `data.bought_on`.
- Pure: `compare_row(L, used, at, hours)` works one light out at `at` inches: PAR and area from `ppfd_at`/`area_at` only when `on_chart()` (within the chart's ends, 6% slack for metric rounding), else `beyond` words; DLI; `per_watt` = PAR × area (m²) / watts (µmol/J, rough); `per_light` = price / (PAR × area) × 100 (dollars per 100 µmol/s, rough); `light_source()` ('maker', 'estimate' from `data.estimated`, 'doubtful' from `data.doubtful` with its reason); `first_num()` for CRI text.
- Routes: `GET /api/lights/compare?at&hours` (rows, choices, statuses, directions, mount kinds, spots); `POST /api/lights/<id>/status {status, count?, on?}` ("Bought it" is status owned); `GET /api/lights/<id>/try?spot=ID` (the spot's plants now and with this light, using `spot_eval` at the spot's distance, hours and nearest brightness step it has, plus `spot_fit` and notes). `POST /api/lights` takes `status: 'considering'` (count forced to 0); `light_fields` (where.py) also takes brand, url, cri, kelvin, price (+ `price_on`, today by default), direction, timer_repeats, timer_keeps, needs_socket. A PUT that gives a count to a light that is not owned is refused: "Bought it" does that.
- Batch C6: `doubtful` on the gooseneck and the 30 W bulb, `timer_keeps: false` on the 80 W bulb and the tripod.
- Front end `js/compare.js` + `css/compare.css` (after `mixes` in the manifest). Page `#/compare` registered with `nav: 'lights'` and a label but no section, so it is not in the top bar (a ninth Care link wraps the row) and Search finds it; the addition `cmp-ltlink` puts a link in Your lights' header. State in `CMP`; columns in `cmpCols()` (label, value in words, short table value `tv` with the unit in the header `tl`, sort value, `hi` for highest-first); filters in `cmpFilters()`; `cmpDraw()` redraws the list, chips and sentence and keeps the focus on the same control. The table's wrapper has class `wide` so `fitTables()` leaves it alone; `cmpFit()` draws cards instead under 900 px or when the table overflows. Settings `at`, `hours`, `sort`, `cols` are in `plantdb.compare`, a synced pref (prefs.js KEYS). Checks: `dev/test/unit/test_compare.py`, `dev/test/compare_check.py`.

## What is in each pot (1.56.0, PLAN.md step 9)
- `app/plantdb/mixes.py` (ORDER 62). Tables (in `MIXES_SCHEMA`, core.py, created by `init_db`): `amendments(key UNIQUE, name, role part|additive|medium, grades JSON list, holds_water 1-5, airy 1-5, lasts_months (0 = does not break down), feeds 0-2, ph -1|0|1, weight 1-3, source, notes, created/updated by/at)`; `mix_recipes(name UNIQUE, notes)` and `mix_parts(recipe_id, amendment_id, grade, parts REAL, amount, sort)`; `pot_mixes(plant_code, care_id (the Repotted entry, or NULL for "What is it in now?"), mixed_on, recipe_id, parts JSON snapshot, top, bottom JSON layers {amendment_id, name, grade, depth_in}, notes)`. A pot's parts are a snapshot (name and role copied in), so changing or removing a recipe or an amendment never rewrites what a pot was in. The plant's current mix is its newest `pot_mixes` row (`current_mix`).
- Plant fields `grow_kind` ('Soil mix', 'Semi-hydro', 'Water only'; empty means Soil mix, and `leaf_type` 'Grows in water' reads as Water only), `reservoir` ('Drains freely', 'A tray under it', 'A catch pot', 'The pot itself (no holes)') and `mix_wants` ('Fast-draining', 'Balanced', 'Moisture-holding'). `mix_wants` is also a species fact (`FACT_FIELDS`, group Soil); `mix_wants_of()` takes the plant's own value, then the fact.
- Pure: `mix_summary(rows)` (share-weighted water and air over the non-additive parts, given only when half the mix or more has a value; freshening from the soonest-breaking part that is 20% or more; words with "An estimate"), `mix_warning(summary, wants, kind)` (soil mixes only), `parts_words`, `am_words`, `am_low` (a name inside a sentence; LECA keeps its capitals), `median`. `recipe_interval()` is the median per plant of the days between waterings since the plant went into the recipe, over plants whose newest mix is it; shown from three plants and six gaps.
- Routes: `GET /api/mixes` (amendments, recipes with summary, words, plants and interval, the word lists, `every`); `POST /api/mixes/amendments`, `PUT|DELETE /api/mixes/amendments/<id>`; `POST /api/mixes/recipes`, `PUT|DELETE /api/mixes/recipes/<id>` (409 on a name in use); `GET|POST /api/mixes/plant/<code>` (POST: `{mixed_on, recipe_id?, parts?, top?, bottom?, notes?, repot?, save_as?}`; a recipe with no parts given takes the recipe's; `repot` also writes a `repot` care entry); `DELETE /api/mixes/pot/<id>`; `PUT /api/mixes/every` (setting `hydro_every`, days 1 to 90 per kind); `GET /api/mixes/care` (Today: flushes and root checks due or never logged). All are refused for viewers by the usual rule.
- Care kinds `topup`, `flush` (semi-hydro), `waterchange`, `rootcheck` (water only), in `CARE_KINDS`. `hydro_state()` gives each rhythm's last, next and state; a flush or a watering counts as a top-up, a watering as a water change. `water_report()` (care.py) gives every plant `action` (`water`, `topup`, `waterchange`) and `action_word`; semi-hydro and water-only plants count their last top-up, flush or water change and never take rain. The sitter sheet entries carry `action` and `action_words`; `push_due` says "to top up" / "to give fresh water". `repot_mix()` (repotting.py) brings the plan forward when the mix wants freshening before the usual interval.
- Batch C5 (`dev/seed_src/build_batchC5.py`): `amendments_add` (inserted once by key), 67 species `mix_wants` facts read from their care sheets, guarded `updates` for Ethan's pots (grow_kind, reservoir, and P-032's container and soil_medium texts), and `pot_mixes_add` (only for a plant with no pot_mixes yet).
- Front end `js/mixes.js` (+ `css/mixes.css`, after `fit` in the manifest): page `#/mixes` (no link of its own in the top bar: a ninth Care link wraps the row at 1280 wide, which makes the bar tall and turns off Grow lights' sticky title; `nav: 'pots'` marks Pots and trays, the addition `mx-potslink` on #/pots links to it, and its label without a section lets Search find it) with the dialogs New recipe / Change (`mxrecipe`), Add an amendment / Change (`mxam`), parts rows (`mxpartadd`, `mxpartdel`, `mxampick` shows How much for an additive), the form `mxevery`; the plant-page addition `mx-plant` (after the Soil section) with `mxfield` selects (PUT /api/plants with base_version), What is it in now? and Repotted (`mxpot`, `mxrecipepick` fills the parts), Save as a recipe (`mxsaverecipe`), Remove an earlier entry (`mxpotdel`); Today block `mx-hydro` (order 33, after Water). care.js's `HYDRO_DO` gives the watering button and card words per action. Viewers: change buttons in mixes.css's viewer rule; the selects are disabled. Checks: `dev/test/unit/test_mixes.py`, `dev/test/mixes_check.py`.

## Lights that fit the room (1.56.0, PLAN.md step 8)
- `app/plantdb/fit.py` (ORDER 60). A light's **mount** lives in `lights.data['mount']`: `{kind: socket|floor|surface|clamp|under|hang|none, points: down|angled|side, footprint_in, height_lo, height_hi, grip_in, reach_in, length_in, pot_rim}` (`mount_clean`, `mount_words`); `light_fields()` in where.py takes `mount` on `PUT /api/lights/<id>`. Batch C4 (`dev/seed_src/build_batchC4.py`) fills the eleven models with `lights_update {mount: [null, {...}]}`; a number no listing gives is left out.
- A room's or shelf's **place sheet** is JSON in `areas.sheet` / `spots.sheet` (`sheet_clean`: levels, gap_in, width_in, depth_in, top nothing|ceiling|other, top_in, free_in, floor_in, edge yes|no, edge_in, socket yes|no, ceiling_in, notes; `sheet_words`). `GET/PUT /api/rooms/<pid>` (pid: a spot's id, or minus a room's id, as on Grow lights). Photos: table `place_photos(area_id, spot_id NULL for the room itself, file, caption, is_primary, added_by, added_at)` in `PHOTO_DIR` via `save_image`; `POST /api/rooms/<pid>/photos`, `PUT|DELETE /api/rooms/photos/<id>`; `place_photo_map()` gives the main photo per place for Grow lights, Rooms and spots (`where_list`), and the sitter sheet (`places`, served under the share token too). Removing a room or spot removes its photos (`place_photos_drop`). Batches can set a sheet with `place_sheets [{room, name, sheet}]`, only where none is set (where_seed runs first so the places exist).
- A plant's current height is the plant field `height_in` (num, inches; `LEN_FIELDS` in edit.js shows it in the person's unit; the plant page's inline editor and the health walk-through convert too).
- `fit_check(mount, light_name, place, spot_sheet, room_sheet, level, holder, distance_in, plants, others, u)` is pure. The shelf's values override the room's. A bulb (`socket`) takes the mount of its holder (`HOLDERS`: clamp → clamp, base → surface, cord → hang). Lines are `{kind: fits|nofit|tall|shaded|point|unchecked|note, word, text}`; status is nofit if any nofit or tall, else unchecked if any unchecked, else fits; `clear_in` is the clear height over the plants' level, which `better_spot()` uses so "It would suit … better" never names a spot whose light does not fit or whose level the plant is too tall for. Missing data is always an `unchecked` line naming what to fill in. `spot_fit(c, spot, light, plants)` wraps it for a `light_spots` row or a preview dict; `lights_state()` adds `fit`, `checked`, `level`, `holder` and `place_photo` to each spot, `mount`/`mount_words` to each light and `photo`/`words` to each place; `/api/lights/preview` adds `fit`.
- `light_spots` gains `level` ('' | top | lower), `holder` ('' | clamp | base | cord) and `checked_at/by/note/sig`. `spot_sig()` hashes the light, units, place, level, holder, distance and the plants (not the hours or brightness); a stored sig that differs makes `checked.stale`. `POST|DELETE /api/lights/spots/<id>/checked`; `GET /api/lights/unchecked` (spots with plants, not checked or stale) feeds Today.
- Front end `js/fit.js` (+ `css/fit.css`, after `facts` in the manifest): `fitBlock()`, `checkedLine()`, `mountLine()`, `placeBits()` called by lights.js; the dialogs How it holds (`ltmount`, `mountkind` shows the fields a kind has) and Measurements and photos (`ltplace`, `placephoto`, `placephotomain`, `placephotodel`); Set up and checked (`spotcheck`, `spotuncheck`); the page `#/lightcard/<id>` (setup card); Today block `fit-unchecked` (order 74, after Needed from you). The spot dialog has Level and What the bulb screws into (shown only for a socket mount) and a live `#sp-fitbox`. Viewers: the change buttons are in fit.css's viewer rule. Checks: `dev/test/unit/test_fit.py`, `dev/test/mounting_check.py`.
- Between releases (1.56.0): `images.yml` tags a push to main `main` and `main-<sha>` once a release `v<VERSION>` exists, so merging work for the next monthly release never replaces the released version's images. The push that changes VERSION gets the version and `latest`, and release.yml makes the release.

- Levels that differ (1.56.0, November 10): a sheet may hold `gaps_in`, one clear height per level that has another above it, bottom first; a spot's `level` is `top`, `lower` (one gap for all, `gap_in`) or `L1`..`L19` counted from the bottom (`L<levels>` is the top). `level_gap()` picks the height; "a lower level" on a shelf whose heights differ is "Not checked" until a numbered level is chosen. `lights_state` gives each place its `levels` and `gaps_in` for the spot dialog's level list (`spotLevels()` in lights.js). Rooms and spots has the same **Measurements and photos** button (`ltplace`) for every spot, outside ones too.
- Batch handler `place_sheets_update` ({room?, side, name / aka / contains, set: {field: [old, new]}}): field by field, only where the spot still holds the old value, and only when exactly one spot matches. Batch C7 holds Ethan's shelf measurements.
- 1.56.1 (Ethan's notes, November 10): `stack_factor(area, units, need)` in lights.py: several of a light over plants that fit under fewer of them add up. Strength is multiplied by units × area ÷ the larger of one light's area and the pots' room, between 1 and units (units when the light has no area figures); `spot_eval` reports `stack`, `units`, `area_known`, and the area lit as units × area ÷ that factor; `best_setting` uses the same. `crowded` still compares the pots' room with units × area. The spot dialog's level list for a one-level place is just "Its one level"; "Hide plants already in another spot" (`data-sp=hide`, rows' `data-other`, remembered in localStorage `plantdb.sphide`). `/api/where/sort` gives each plant's `photo`. 1.56.3: the section is "Plants without a spot": GET groups every active plant with no spot by (written words or none, area) and suggests a spot already there (`spot_id`) when the words name one; POST items `{text, from_area_id, area_id, spot_id | name, codes?}` match the plants in `from_area_id` (the area they are in now; matching on the chosen area was why moving to another room did nothing), use a spot of that name in any case, and say which spots were `made`. Front end: `whsCard()`, `whsSpotOpts()`, `whsItem()` in core.js; acts `whsone`, `whsort`.
