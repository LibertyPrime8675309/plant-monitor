Plant updates from Claude
=========================

Claude saves a file here when it has corrections, photos or new pages for your plants.
Its name starts with "plant-updates" and ends in .json or .zip.

The app reads this folder and never changes it. When a file is waiting, Today says
so, and the page "Updates from Claude" (in the Plants menu) shows every change in it.
Nothing in your record is changed until you press the button on that page.

A file is brought in once. After that you can delete it from this folder, or leave it:
the app remembers that it was brought in and does not offer it again.

These files hold details of your plants. They stay on this computer: they are not in
a release and not on GitHub.
