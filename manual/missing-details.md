# Missing details

Part of the Plant record manual. The README in the folder above has how to start, stop, update and back up the app, and the list of every manual page.

## Missing details

Some pages need a detail about a plant to work for it, and quietly leave the plant out or use a guess without it. **Missing details** (the link on **All plants**, or Search for "missing") lists, detail by detail, the active plants at the home you are using that do not have it yet:

- **Pot size**: grow-light spacing and the coming-inside checklist.
- **Leaf type**: the coming-inside checklist and the repotting plan.
- **Height with its pot**: shelves and grow light spots.
- **Coldest it can take**: the frost alert and the cold-night checklist.
- **How often to check for water**: Water.
- **Feeding months**: the feeding calendar.
- **Safety for pets**: the pets filter.
- Each plant has a box and a **Save** button; Enter saves too, and the keyboard then moves to the next plant in the list. Heights and temperatures are typed in your own units. **Feeding months** has **Open Edit** instead, since it is three boxes.
- Saving is an ordinary edit of the plant's page: it shows in Page history and the Audit log. If someone else changed the plant in the meantime nothing is overwritten.
- People who can only view see the lists but not the boxes.
- **Since November 15, 2026** the list also has **Light**, **Potting mix** (the button opens the plant's page, where What is in each pot records it) and **Date acquired** (a date box).
- **Not now** beside a plant leaves it off that one list for 30 days, for a detail you do not know yet. Nothing on its page changes. The list says how many are left off, and **Show them again** brings them back.
- A long list shows ten plants at a time, with **Show 10 more**.
- A plant's own page says what it is missing, under its name and care buttons, with **Fill them in**.
