# Your own reminders (prototype)

Part of the Plant record manual. The README in the folder above has how to start, stop, update and back up the app, and the list of every manual page.

## Your own reminders (prototype)

A note to yourself with a date: "Check the cutting for roots", "Spray again for mealybugs in 5 days", "Buy potting mix". **Today → Reminders** lists them.

- **Add a reminder** on the Reminders page (for one plant, or **Not for a particular plant**), on Today, or on a plant's page under the care buttons (for that plant). Say what to do, then when: tomorrow, in 3 days, in a week, in 2 weeks, in a month, in 3 months, or on a date you choose.
- **Repeat:** a reminder can come back every week, 2 weeks, month, 2 months, 3 months, 6 months or year. It comes back that long after the day you press **Done**, not after the day it was due.
- **On Today:** a **Reminders** section and a **Reminders (N)** jump button from the day a reminder is due until you press **Done**. One past its day says "Late:" and how many days.
- **Each reminder** has **Done**, **A week later** (moves it to a week from today) and **Change** (the words, the date, how often it repeats, or **Remove it**).
- **Done lately** on the Reminders page lists the last 30 days, with **Not done yet** to put one back.
- Every reminder added, moved, done or removed is in the Audit log, with the plant it is for. People who can only view see reminders but not the buttons. Reminders follow the home you are looking at.
- **Since November 15, 2026:** a late or due reminder has **Tomorrow** and **A week later**; the Today block sits near the top, before Water; your phone gets one message at 8 in the morning when reminders are due (turn "Your own reminders" off under Notifications if you do not want it); **Done** works with no connection and is sent later; reminders are in the calendar file and in the JSON download.
